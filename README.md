## About This Project

The Plan of Salvation webpage was created as an outreach resource for Bethesda Tabernacle to share the biblical message of salvation through Jesus Christ.

This landing page is designed to help visitors understand the New Birth experience through:

- Repentance
- Water Baptism in the Name of Jesus Christ
- Receiving the Gift of the Holy Ghost

The foundation of this message is found in Acts 2:37-38.

---

## Purpose

This page serves as a digital resource for:

- Facebook outreach
- QR code ministry materials
- Visitors seeking answers about salvation
- Sharing the Gospel message online

The goal is to provide a clear and welcoming presentation of the Plan of Salvation and provide a connection point to Bethesda Tabernacle.

## Biblical Foundation

### Acts 2:37-38
