God's Plan For Salvation

We are all perfectly imperfect people in need of a Savior. Because of sin, every person needs to experience the transforming power of Jesus Christ and become spiritually born again.

Through salvation, we become part of God's family and receive access to His promises, including the promise of eternal life. Salvation is not simply becoming a better person; it is a complete transformation through Jesus Christ.

The New Birth Experience

Jesus taught that we must be born again. The new birth is a spiritual transformation that takes place through repentance, water baptism in the Name of Jesus Christ, and receiving the gift of the Holy Ghost.

After the resurrection of Jesus Christ, the Apostles preached the message of salvation. On the Day of Pentecost, those who heard the Word were convicted in their hearts and asked what they needed to do.

The Apostolic Plan of Salvation

Acts 2:37

"Now when they heard this, they were pricked in their heart, and said unto Peter and to the rest of the apostles, Men and brethren, what shall we do?"

Acts 2:38

"Then Peter said unto them, Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost."

1. Repentance

Repentance is a change of heart, mind, and direction. It is turning away from sin and turning toward God, surrendering our lives completely to Jesus Christ.

True repentance prepares our hearts to receive God's forgiveness and begin a new life in Him.

2. Water Baptism in Jesus' Name

Water baptism is an act of faith and obedience. Through baptism, we identify with the death, burial, and resurrection of Jesus Christ.

The Apostles baptized believers in the Name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins.

"And they were baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus."
Acts 19:5

3. The Gift of the Holy Ghost

The Holy Ghost is God's promise to believers. Through His Spirit, God gives power, guidance, and a transformed life.

"And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance."
Acts 2:4

There Is Salvation In The Name Of Jesus

The name of Jesus Christ is the name above every name. Through His name we receive forgiveness, salvation, and new life.

"Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved."
Acts 4:12

Begin Your New Life In Christ

Jesus Christ is calling every person into a relationship with Him. Through repentance, baptism in the Name of Jesus Christ, and receiving the Holy Ghost, you can experience the promise of salvation and a transformed life.
